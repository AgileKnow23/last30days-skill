Serper results labeled with relative dates (`10 hours ago`, `5 days ago`, `2 weeks ago`) are now resolved against the UTC retrieval instant and kept when their whole plausible interval falls inside the research window, instead of being discarded as undated; each web result carries `date_provenance` (`source_absolute`, `derived_relative`, `unknown`) and the grounding artifact records why results were dropped. YouTube publication dates from ScrapeCreators (`publishedTime`) and yt-dlp (`upload_date`, `timestamp`) are preserved with the same provenance; unknown dates stay null and never count as fresh. The per-subquery web result count is configurable via `LAST30DAYS_WEB_MAX_RESULTS` / `--web-max-results` (default 5, bounds 1-20). New `--investment-topic` flag and `lib/investment_topic.py` establish the `<Company Name> $<TICKER> <short research objective>` contract for listed-company topics, with a non-blocking warning for financial topics that lead with the symbol (a bare ticker, or a cashtag before the company name). The agent JSON export is now `schema_version` `1.3` (adds `date_provenance` per result).
